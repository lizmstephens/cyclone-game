For each storm, the build preserves original WATTCH initialisations, interpolates missing daily forecasts between adjacent original runs and proposes a shorter playing window based on the Madagascar threat. The original tracks remain available after the playing window ends.

Review priorities: scenarios shorter than five briefings, scenarios with no detected Madagascar threat, missing daily reconstruction, and storms with a possible later return.
