Madagascar Cyclone Anticipatory Action Game

Manage a shared budget for cyclone anticipatory action. Decide when to keep monitoring, pre-activate preparedness, and fully activate early actions in threatened areas.

Play one storm or an unknown number of storms in a fictional season. Real identities are revealed only at the end. Daily briefings are training simulations based on WATTCH hindcasts.
